ACME CORP - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Go to Settings > Security > Reset Password. Enter your email address and we will send you a reset link within 5 minutes. The link expires after 24 hours.

Q: What is your refund policy?
A: We offer a full refund within 30 days of purchase. To request a refund, contact support@acmecorp.com with your order number. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Go to Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription. Your subscription will remain active until the end of your current billing period. You will not be charged again.

Q: How do I contact customer support?
A: You can reach us at support@acmecorp.com or call 1-800-555-0199. Our business hours are Monday-Friday, 9 AM - 6 PM EST.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. All payments are processed securely through Stripe.

Q: How do I upgrade my plan?
A: Go to Settings > Billing > Change Plan. Select your desired plan and the upgrade will take effect immediately. You will be charged the prorated difference.

Q: Is my data secure?
A: Yes. We use AES-256 encryption for all data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit. We are SOC2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant.

Q: Do you offer a free trial?
A: Yes, we offer a 14-day free trial for all new customers. No credit card required. You can upgrade to a paid plan at any time during or after the trial.
